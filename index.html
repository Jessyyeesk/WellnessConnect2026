Wellness Connect

Join Dr. Giggles in a wacky race through Wellness Town. Hold the gas, steer into the gate with the right answer and learn how to look after your blood pressure, blood sugar and mind.

What you will learn

  • Signs, risks and healthy habits for high blood pressure and diabetes
  • What stress, anxiety and depression look like
  • Practical coping skills and when to ask for help

15 questions · about 15 minutes · ages 6 to 70 · no timer pressure

Educational game only. It cannot diagnose anyone. If you are worried about your health, speak to a doctor or counsellor. In an emergency in Malaysia, call 999.

How to play

Race to the right answer

Read the question and answers A to D. Your car only moves when you speed up, so hold gas to race up the track and steer into the gate with the answer you choose. Crossing the finish line locks in your answer.

Keyboard: ↑ or W = gas, ↓ or S = brake, ← → or A / D = steer, 1 to 4 = jump to a lane.

Mobile: hold the GAS button, tap BRAKE, use ◀ ▶ or tap a lane.

Scoring

10 points for each correct answer on the first try. Driving speed, crashes and time never change your score, only your answer does. You can pause driving at any time by letting go of the gas.

After each race you see an explanation. Press Continue when ready. At the end you get a star rating and can retry missed questions for practice.

Untimed quiz mode removes the driving and uses simple multiple choice.
