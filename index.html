Wellness Connect

Join Dr. Giggles on a wild day at Wellness Town. Shoot targets, race through gates and solve real-life health puzzles, all while learning how to look after your body and mind.

What you will learn

  • Why physical and mental health both matter
  • How to take care of yourself every day
  • Warning signs of major depressive disorder, other mood disorders and schizophrenia, and when to ask for help

15 questions · about 15 minutes · ages 6 to 70 · no timer pressure

Educational game only. It cannot diagnose anyone. If you are worried about your health, speak to a doctor or counsellor. In an emergency in Malaysia, call 999.

How to play

Three kinds of rounds

🎯 Shooting: answers are on moving signs. Click or tap one to shoot it. Keyboard: press 1, 2, 3 or 4.

🚗 Racing: read the answers (A to D), press Start engine, then steer into your gate. Keyboard: ← → or A / D. Mobile: tap a lane or the arrow buttons.

🧩 Problem solving: read the situation and pick the best choice. Click, tap, or press 1 to 4.

Scoring

Each question is worth 10 points if you get it right on the first try. Missing a shot or crashing never costs points: only your answer counts, not your speed or aim.

After each answer you see an explanation. Press Continue when you are ready. At the end you get a star rating and can retry the questions you missed for practice.

Untimed focus mode turns every round into simple multiple choice.
